DAY 17 • EXCEL MASTERY

PivotTables Basics

Learn how to summarize large amounts of Excel data quickly using PivotTables.

🎯 Today's Goal

Understand what PivotTables are, why they are useful, and how to create a basic PivotTable from an Excel data table.

💡 What is a PivotTable?

A PivotTable is an Excel tool that helps you summarize, analyze and organize large datasets without changing the original data.

💡 Simple Example: If you have thousands of sales records, a PivotTable can quickly show total sales by department, employee or month.

📚 What You Will Learn

📊 Create PivotTable

Convert a normal data table into a PivotTable.

📝 Rows

Use fields in Rows to group similar records together.

🔢 Values

Calculate Sum, Count, Average and other summaries.

🔍 Filters

Filter the PivotTable to focus on specific information.

🛠️ How to Create a PivotTable

1

Select your complete dataset including the column headings.

2

Go to the Insert tab in Excel.

3

Click PivotTable.

4

Select the location where you want the PivotTable.

5

Drag fields into Rows, Columns, Values or Filters.

🎯 Understanding PivotTable Areas

Rows

Groups your data into categories. Example: Department.

Columns

Displays categories horizontally. Example: Month.

Values

Performs calculations such as Sum or Average.

Filters

Allows you to display only selected portions of the data.

📈 Example PivotTable

Suppose you want to find total sales by department.

Department Total Sales
IT 120000
Sales 45000

The original dataset can contain hundreds or thousands of rows, but the PivotTable summarizes it into a simple report.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

❌ Missing Headers

Every column should have a clear heading.

❌ Blank Rows

Avoid unnecessary blank rows inside your dataset.

❌ Wrong Field Area

Place fields in the correct PivotTable areas.

❌ Forgetting Refresh

Refresh your PivotTable when the source data changes.

📝 Practice Task

Create a PivotTable that shows Total Sales by Department.


Then add Month to the Columns area and observe how the report changes.


Finally, add Department to the Filter area and practice filtering your report.
