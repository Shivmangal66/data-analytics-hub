🔢 Useful Formulas

Total Sales:
=SUM(F2:F101)

Total Orders:
=COUNTA(A2:A101)

Average Sales:
=AVERAGE(F2:F101)

Maximum Sale:
=MAX(F2:F101)

Minimum Sale:
=MIN(F2:F101)

💼 Real-World Dashboard

A professional Sales Dashboard can help a company answer questions such as:


📌 How much did we sell?


📌 Which product sells the most?


📌 Which region performs best?


📌 How are sales changing over time?


📌 Which category contributes the most revenue?

⚠️ Dashboard Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Too Many Charts

Use only charts that communicate useful information.

❌ Too Many Colors

Keep the dashboard clean and professional.

❌ No KPIs

Important numbers should be visible at a glance.

❌ Poor Alignment

Keep charts and KPI cards properly aligned.
