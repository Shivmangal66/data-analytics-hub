📚 What You Will Learn

📊 Column Chart

🥧 Pie Chart

Show how different categories contribute to a total.

📉 Bar Chart

Compare categories using horizontal bars.

💡 What is Data Visualization?

Data Visualization means representing data visually using charts, graphs and other graphical elements.

💡 A good chart can make a large dataset much easier to understand than a table full of numbers.

🛠️ How to Create a Chart

1

Select your data including the headings.

2

Go to the Insert tab.

3

Choose the required chart type.

4

Add a meaningful chart title.

5

Adjust labels, legend and formatting.

📊 Column Chart

A Column Chart uses vertical columns to compare values across categories.

Best for: Sales by Month, Revenue by Product, Students by Course

📈 Line Chart

A Line Chart connects data points with lines and is especially useful for showing trends over time.

Best for: Monthly Sales, Stock Prices, Website Traffic, Revenue Trends

🥧 Pie Chart

A Pie Chart displays the proportion of each category as a part of a whole.

Best for: Market Share, Expense Distribution, Product Contribution

📉 Bar Chart

A Bar Chart uses horizontal bars and is useful when category names are long.

Best for: Department Performance, Product Ranking, Employee Comparison

🎯 Choosing the Right Chart

Purpose Recommended Chart
Compare Categories Column / Bar
Show Trend Line
Show Proportion Pie
Show Ranking Bar

🎨 Professional Chart Tips

1️⃣ Clear Title

Give every chart a meaningful title that explains what it shows.

2️⃣ Keep It Simple

Avoid unnecessary elements that make the chart difficult to read.

3️⃣ Correct Chart

Select a chart type that matches the purpose of your analysis.

4️⃣ Readable Labels

Make sure labels and values are easy to understand.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

❌ Wrong Chart

Do not use a Pie Chart when comparing many categories.

❌ Too Much Data

Avoid putting too many categories into one chart.

❌ No Title

Always give your chart a clear title.

❌ Poor Formatting

Keep fonts, labels and spacing professional and readable.
