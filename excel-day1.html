📌 What is Excel?

Excel is a spreadsheet software used to store, organize, calculate and analyze data.

Today You Will Learn

  • What is Excel?
  • What is a Workbook?
  • What is a Worksheet?
  • Rows and Columns
  • Cells and Cell Address
  • Basic Excel Interface

📚 Important Terms

Workbook: An Excel file.

Worksheet: A sheet inside a workbook.

Cell: The intersection of a row and column.

Cell Address: The location of a cell, such as A1.

Row: Horizontal arrangement of cells.

Column: Vertical arrangement of cells.

💡 Example

If you enter Shiv in cell A1, then the cell address is A1.

If you enter 100 in cell B1, then the cell address is B1.
