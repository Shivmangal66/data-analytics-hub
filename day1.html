DAY 01 • EXCEL MASTERY

Getting to Know Excel

Welcome to Day 1 of your Excel learning journey. Today you will become familiar with the Excel interface, cells, workbooks and worksheets.

🎯 Today's Goal

Understand the basic Excel environment and learn how workbooks, worksheets and cells are organized.

📚 What You Will Learn

🖥️ Excel Interface

Understand the basic areas and controls you see when working in Excel.

📁 Workbook

A workbook is the Excel file in which your worksheets are stored.

📄 Worksheet

A worksheet is the individual spreadsheet area inside a workbook.

🔲 Cells

Cells are the individual boxes where you enter and work with data.

💡 Understanding the Structure

Think of Excel like a notebook:

📁 Workbook = Your complete Excel file

📄 Worksheet = A sheet inside the workbook

🔲 Cell = An individual location where data is entered

📝 Practice Task

Open Excel and explore the workbook, worksheet and cells. Click different cells and observe how their cell references change.
